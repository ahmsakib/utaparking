Your first 6–8 weeks

Week 1: one working set per exercise. Week 2 onward: two sets if you recover comfortably. Train on three separate days; walk or rest between sessions.

Use 2 minutes of rest for presses, rows and leg exercises; 60–90 seconds for raises, curls and core. Keep 2–3 good reps in reserve.

✓ Progress and weight notes save on this device. Dumbbells: log the weight of one dumbbell. Sets start fresh each day.

BEFORE YOU BEGIN

Find your rhythm.

  1. Warm upWalk or cycle for 5–10 minutes. Add light practice sets before your first big exercises.
  2. Keep something in the tankFinish each set feeling you could do 2–3 more good reps.
  3. Earn the next weightReach the top reps on every set for two sessions, then add the smallest weight increase.
